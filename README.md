# Instoryview Story Viewer

**A free Instagram story viewer for public accounts.** Enter a username to watch current stories, browse highlights and posts, and see profile details in your browser, with nothing to install. Part of [Instoryview](https://instoryview.com).

**Open it:** https://instoryview.com/stories

## What you can do

- **Watch stories** of any public account. Profiles that are private cannot be viewed.
- **Browse highlights and posts**, with reels, reposts and tagged posts, laid out like an Instagram profile.
- **See profile details:** bio, post / follower / following counts, "About this account" and suggested accounts.
- **Work on any device:** phone or desktop browser, no app and no extension.
- **Keep it private:** searches are not saved to a shareable URL, and recent searches stay in your own browser.

## How it works

1. Open [instoryview.com/stories](https://instoryview.com/stories).
2. Type a public Instagram username.
3. Watch the stories. Sign in (free) to open highlights, posts, comments and follower / following lists.

Anonymous use is covered by a small daily allowance of free credits per visitor; signing in adds more.

## FAQ

**Will the account know I viewed their story?** Instoryview is an independent viewer that does not log in to Instagram as you, so Instagram does not show your own account in the story's viewer list.

**Can I view private accounts?** No. Only public content is available.

**Is it affiliated with Instagram or Meta?** No. Instoryview is independent and not endorsed by Instagram or Meta.

**Can I get an account removed?** Yes. Account owners can remove their account at [instoryview.com/removal](https://instoryview.com/removal).

**Want to see who followed or unfollowed someone over time?** Use the [Instoryview follower tracker](https://instoryview.com).
