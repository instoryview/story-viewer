# Instoryview Story Viewer

A free Instagram story viewer for public accounts: look up a username and watch their current stories, highlights and posts from a browser, without the account knowing.

**Use it:** https://instoryview.com/stories

- Works on phone and desktop, in the browser. No app to install.
- Public accounts only. Private accounts can't be viewed.
- Searches are not saved to a shareable URL.
- Daily free credits apply; signing in unlocks highlights, posts and follower lists.

Want to know who followed or unfollowed an account over time? That's the [Instoryview follower tracker](https://instoryview.com).

> This repository is the public home of the tool: issues, feedback and feature requests. The service itself is hosted at instoryview.com and is not open source.

## Feedback

Open an [issue](https://github.com/instoryview/story-viewer/issues) or write to support@instoryview.com. To have an account removed from Instoryview, use https://instoryview.com/removal.
